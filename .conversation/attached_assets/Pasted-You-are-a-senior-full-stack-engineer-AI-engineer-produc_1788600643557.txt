7. INVENTORY CALCULATIONS
====================================================

Implement real business logic.

Days Remaining:

current_stock / average_daily_sales

Stock Risk:

IF days_remaining < supplier_lead_time
THEN HIGH RISK

IF days_remaining >= supplier_lead_time
AND days_remaining <= supplier_lead_time + 3
THEN MEDIUM RISK

ELSE LOW RISK

Overstock:

IF days_remaining > 60
THEN OVERSTOCK

Sales Growth:

(current_period_sales - previous_period_sales)
/
previous_period_sales * 100

Slow Moving:

Identify products with unusually low sales velocity.

Keep this business logic separate from the UI.
